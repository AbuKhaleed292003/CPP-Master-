Security Principles & Practices
Sources: ASIS POA
2018 Domain Review
🔐 D1 · Key Facts & Concepts

Systems Approach (3 Elements)

  • Assessment of Vulnerability → Implementation of Countermeasures → Evaluation of Effectiveness
  • Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Consequence
  • Residual risk = risk AFTER countermeasures · ALE = SLE × ARO

ASIS GSRA — 9 Steps (start: understand the org)

  • CARVER: Criticality · Accessibility · Recuperability · Vulnerability · Effect · Recognizability
  • Quantitative = numerical values · Qualitative = descriptive scales H/M/L
  • SWOT: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats

PPS Three Functions

  • Detection · Delay · Response
  • Response Time MUST be less than adversary task time after detection

VA Team (6 Members)

  • Security Specialist (LEADER) · Systems Engineer · Response Expert · Data Analyst · Ops Rep · SMEs

Risk Management Strategies

  • Avoidance · Transfer (insurance) · Spreading · Reduction · Acceptance

Key Formulas

  • K = (CP+CT+CR+Ci)–(I–a) · Span of Control: greater sophistication → NARROWER span

Business Principles & Practices
Sources: ASIS POA
2018 Domain Review
💼 D2 · Key Facts & Concepts

McGregor Theory X vs Y

  • Theory X: workers lazy, avoid work, need supervision, motivated by fear
  • Theory Y: naturally motivated, want to work — MORE SUCCESSFUL approach

Herzberg Motivation-Hygiene

  • Motivators (job content): Achievement, Recognition, Responsibility, Advancement
  • Hygienes (job context): Salary, Working conditions, Company policies, Coworkers

Financial Ratios

  • Gross Profit Margin = (Revenue–COGS–G&A) / Revenue
  • Operating Margin = EBITA / Revenue · Net Margin = Net Income / Revenue
  • ROA = Net Income / Total Assets · ROE = Net Income / Shareholder Equity

Fraud Triangle — ALL THREE required

  • Financial Pressure + Opportunity + Rationalization
  • Predominant factor: GREED · Employee situational red flags: debt, living beyond means

Budget Types & Key Rules

  • Line Item · Program · Capital · Zero-Based
  • Personnel costs = LARGEST item · Security Mgr sets compensation — NOT purchasing dept
  • Frog Syndrome: manager starts training → abandons when workload increases

🔍 D3 · Key Facts & Concepts

Investigation Attributes (5) — ASIS POA

  • Objectivity · Thoroughness · Relevance · Accuracy · Timeliness

Principal Resources (5)

  • People · Information · Credibility · Physical assets · Financial assets

Two Key Outsourcing Decisions

  • (1) WHETHER to outsource · (2) WHICH vendor to select

Top 3 Investigation Techniques

  • Informants · Surveillance · Undercover

Undercover Operations

  • Cover stories · Backstopping (support cover) · Communications protocols · Counter-surveillance
  • Carding = keeping systematic data about information sources

Legal Principles

  • Law of Torts: intentional AND negligent acts
  • Chain of Custody: every handler documented from acquisition to disposition
  • Citizen arrest = while FINDING someone committing an offence (not past suspicion)
  • Hardest international investigation challenge: Cultural differences

Personnel Security
Sources: ASIS POA
2018 Domain Review
👤 D4 · Key Facts & Concepts

Screening Priority

  • Background = MOST IMPORTANT · Interview = Heart of selection · Most expensive = LAST
  • Verify minimum 5 consecutive immediately preceding years of employment
  • Due diligence = reasonable, defensible verification steps
  • Negligent hiring = fail to screen → employee harms someone → employer liable

Testing

  • MMPI: psychological suitability · EPPA: generally prohibits private sector polygraph
  • EMIT = initial drug screen · GC/MS = confirming test · ALWAYS confirm before personnel action

Security Officer Performance

  • Courtesy · Restraint · Interest · First contact = company image
  • Necessary Human Being: Cognitive (knowledge) + Psychomotor (physical) + Affective (attitude)

Key Statistics

  • 80% of computer security incidents = insider attacks

Executive Protection

  • Foundation: THREAT ASSESSMENT first · Most difficult: convincing executive of the need
  • Termination: revoke ALL access BEFORE notifying employee

Physical Security
Sources: ASIS POA
2018 Domain Review
🏢 D5 · Key Facts & Concepts

3 Lines of Defense

  • 1st: Perimeter · 2nd: Building exterior · 3rd: Interior spaces
  • Balanced protection: ALL paths to asset have equal protection — no weak link

PPS Time Principle

  • Response Force Time MUST be less than adversary task time
  • Standoff distance = MOST effective against vehicle-borne threats
  • K-ratings: vehicle weight × speed the barrier can stop (K12 = 15,000 lbs @ 50 mph)

Fencing Standards

  • 7 feet high (excl. top guard) · 9 gauge or heavier · Max 2-inch mesh openings
  • Clear zone: 20 feet INSIDE and 20 feet OUTSIDE fence

5 Lock Types

  • Warded (weakest/oldest) · Lever · Pin Tumbler (MOST COMMON in US) · Wafer · Dial

Sensors

  • PIR: senses HEAT — does NOT emit radiation · Microwave: EMITS — ideal for large outdoor areas
  • Duress alarm: hidden alarm — signals coercion without alerting perpetrator

CPTED (4 Strategies)

  • Natural Surveillance · Natural Access Control · Territorial Reinforcement · Maintenance

Key Numbers

  • 95–99% alarms = false · 0.2 ft-candles perimeter minimum · 4.5–5 persons per 24/7 post
  • Magnetometer: CANNOT detect Copper, Aluminium, Zinc

Information Security
Sources: ASIS POA
2018 Domain Review
💻 D6 · Key Facts & Concepts

CIA Triad

  • Confidentiality (authorized access only) · Integrity (accurate, unaltered) · Availability (accessible when needed)

Key Security Principles

  • Non-repudiation: prove specific party sent/received · Least Privilege: minimum access needed
  • MFA: two+ factors from DIFFERENT categories (Know/Have/Are)
  • Zero-day: unknown to vendor, no patch · Ransomware: encrypts + demands payment

Trade Secret Requirements (3)

  • Competitive advantage · Must be SECRET · Used in business · Published = DISQUALIFIED

Information Loss

  • #1 cause: Inadvertent disclosure (MOST COMMON)
  • Greatest vulnerability = EMPLOYEES with legitimate access

Eavesdropping Methods

  • Wiretapping (wire entry) · Bugging (wireless device) · Pen register (numbers only, no content)
  • 2 reliable protections: Don't discuss sensitive info + Use encryption equipment

Key Numbers

  • Vital records = 2% of total corporate records · TEMPEST = electromagnetic emanation shielding

Crisis Management
Sources: ASIS POA
2018 Domain Review
🚨 D7 · Key Facts & Concepts

Bombing — 4 Required Elements

  • Motive · Material · Knowledge · Opportunity (ONLY controllable element)
  • Most common terrorist tactic = Bombing

Business Continuity

  • BCP: ALL critical functions · DRP: IT systems specifically · BIA must precede BCP
  • RTO: maximum acceptable downtime (time) · RPO: maximum acceptable data loss (time)
  • Hot site: immediate use (most expensive) · Warm site: partial · Cold site: empty (cheapest)

Five Rs of Threat (in order)

  • Receive → Record → Report → Review → Respond

Emergency Plan Requirements

  • Tested ANNUALLY · Directive in nature · 3 elements: Authority / Types / Execution
  • Minimum evacuation distance: 300 metres (328 yards)

Strike Types

  • Economic = 60 days notice · Wildcat = unauthorized · Unfair Labor Practice = sudden

ICS & Exercises

  • Incident Commander = HIGHEST authority at incident scene
  • Table-top = discussion-based · Full-scale = physical deployment
